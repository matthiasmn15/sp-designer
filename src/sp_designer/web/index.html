    Electrode design

    Milestone 2 – next

    Spark plug types

    • Multi-pad – several rectangular precious-metal pads facing each other
    • J-gap – one electrode pair, round or rectangular pads
    • Annular – circular electrodes, cylindrical surfaces facing each other

    Per electrode

    • Precious metal (from the material database) and thickness
    • Pad dimensions, carrier material, optional Cu core
    • Initial gap

    Results

    • Facing areas; wear area = smallest common area of centre and ground electrode
    • Precious metal volume, mass and cost per plug
    • Side-by-side comparison of design variants

    Lifetime

    Milestone 3

    Inputs

    • Combinations of electrode designs and materials
    • Wear per cycle per material, separately for centre and ground electrode (manual now – entered under Materials → Database; calculated later)
    • Ignition voltage vs. gap – manual table for now
    • Available voltage, reserve, engine speed, operating hours

    Results

    • Gap growth and ignition voltage over operating hours
    • Re-gap interval (voltage limit) and lifetime (wear limit)
    • Remaining precious metal at end of life, cost per operating hour

    Spark simulation

    Milestone 4 / research

    0-D model

    • Ignition coil secondary circuit and energy balance
    • Breakdown voltage (Paschen-type, calibratable)
    • Voltage and current traces, energy per phase

    1-D model (research)

    • Spark channel stretching by flow
    • Restrikes and short circuits
    • Energy to the electrodes → input for the wear model

    See docs/research/spark-simulation.md for the model survey.
